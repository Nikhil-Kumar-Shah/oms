Server-Side Event & Readiness Rules

  • Automatic Checklist Initialization: Every new event auto-creates 8 structured readiness checkpoints across categories (PLANNING, COORDINATION, TECHNICAL_PREPARATION, etc.).

  • Server-Authoritative Lifecycle: Transitions progress through PLANNING → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED → ARCHIVED.
  • Zero Hard Deletion: Events and teams remain permanently preserved in PostgreSQL.
