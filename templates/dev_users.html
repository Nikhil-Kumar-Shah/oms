Zero Hard-Deletion Policy

In accordance with the OMS core architectural rules:

  • Users are never hard-deleted from PostgreSQL.
  • Disabling an account transitions its status to DISABLED, sets disabled_at timestamp, and immediately revokes all active sessions.
  • Historical audit logs, vertical assignments, and records remain completely intact.
